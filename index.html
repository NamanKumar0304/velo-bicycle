Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about custom builds, delivery, and warranty.

How does a Gates Carbon Belt Drive compare to a traditional chain?
A Gates Carbon CDX belt requires zero oil or lubrication, which means it will never leave black grease stains on your trousers or office apparel. It cannot rust, operates in near-total silence, and lasts 3 to 4 times longer than a standard bicycle chain (over 25,000 km).
How difficult is assembly when my bike arrives?
Your bicycle arrives 98% pre-assembled and fully tuned in an oversized reinforced shipping carton. All you need to do is install the pedals and straighten the handlebars. We include all premium custom hex tools and a step-by-step video guide. It takes under 10 minutes.
What is your warranty and trial period?
We provide a 5-Year Craftsmanship Warranty on all frames and forks, and a 2-Year Warranty on all accessories and components. We also offer a 30-Day Commute Trial: if you don't love your bicycle after 30 days of daily riding, we'll arrange a return with full refund.
How does this website work with zero JavaScript?
This entire storefront is engineered with modern semantic HTML5 and CSS3 features like CSS :has(), sibling selectors, CSS variables, and native :target modals. It loads instantly with zero console errors, zero battery drain, and works in 100% of modern web browsers.
